The Tuileries neighborhood

Le Florentin sits at the heart of one of Paris's most beautiful neighborhoods — between Place de la Concorde, the Tuileries Gardens, Rue Saint-Honoré and Rue de Rivoli. A prime location before or after visiting the Louvre, the Orangerie Museum, or a stroll along the Champs-Élysées.
